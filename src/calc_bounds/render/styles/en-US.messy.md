- Written fast by a busy ED clinician at the end of a shift: realistic, messy documentation.
- Mostly telegraphic fragments, run-on lines, few or no section headings; items may appear
  out of the usual order (e.g. exam findings inside the HPI, meds at the end).
- Heavy abbreviation and shorthand (pt, c/o, w/, s/p, h/o, r/o, ?PE, ↑, ↓, +, -, x2, neg,
  WNL for unrelated systems, NAD).
- Occasional typos and missing punctuation in ordinary words, but never inside numbers or
  units.
- Some templated or copy-forward text about unrelated organ systems (e.g. a long normal
  review of systems for skin, GU, neuro-psych) and a pasted list of unrelated home
  medications, as long as none of it touches a fact-sheet item.
- Mix of US conventional units, except where the fact sheet gives a unit.
